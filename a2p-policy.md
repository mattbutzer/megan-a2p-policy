## Description

## Opt-In Flow

Recipients may opt in to receive messages from MeganBot through the MeganBot website or by initiating consent through SMS. Consent must be provided directly by the recipient before MeganBot sends automated reminders, scheduling notifications, or follow-up messages.

Providing a phone number for another purpose does not, by itself, constitute consent to receive automated SMS messages. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

## Message Flow

### Website Opt-In

1. The recipient visits https://mattbutzer.github.io/megan-a2p-policy/ and opens the SMS opt-in form.
2. The recipient enters their own mobile phone number.
3. The recipient reviews the following SMS consent disclosure:

   > By checking this box, I agree to receive automated SMS messages from MeganBot at the phone number provided, including appointment reminders, scheduling notifications, confirmations, and follow-up messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See the [Privacy Policy](https://mattbutzer.github.io/megan-a2p-policy/privacy.html) and [Terms and Conditions](https://mattbutzer.github.io/megan-a2p-policy/terms.html).

4. The recipient actively checks an unchecked consent box. The checkbox is not preselected.
5. The recipient submits the form. The date, time, phone number, disclosure version, and consent action are recorded.
6. MeganBot sends a confirmation message such as:

   > MeganBot: You are subscribed to receive reminders, scheduling notifications, confirmations, and follow-up messages. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to cancel. Msg & data rates may apply.

7. The recipient's consent record is stored and available for audit.

### SMS-Initiated Opt-In

1. The recipient sends a text message containing a keyword such as START or SUBSCRIBE to +1 (405) 467-3588.
2. MeganBot responds with a confirmation message and a description of the message types the recipient will receive.
3. The recipient's phone number, opt-in keyword, timestamp, and the response sent are recorded.
4. The recipient may reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe.

## Consent Documentation

For each opt-in event, the following information is recorded and retained:
- **Phone number** of the recipient
- **Date and time** of opt-in (UTC)
- **Method of opt-in** (website form submission or SMS keyword)
- **Disclosure text** presented to the recipient at the time of opt-in
- **IP address** (for website opt-ins)
- **Consent status** (active or revoked)

Consent records are retained for the duration of the recipient's subscription plus 12 months after opt-out.

## Message Frequency

- **Primary recipient:** Occasional reminders, briefings, and task notifications during active hours
- **Third-party contacts:** Infrequent — only for scheduling, reservations, and coordination as needed
- **Active hours:** 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM CDT
- **Silent hours:** Messages are not sent between 10:00 PM and 9:00 AM CDT

## STOP / HELP / Keywords

- **STOP** — Opt out of all SMS messages from this number
- **HELP** — Display help information with contact details
- **UNSTOP** — Resume receiving messages after STOP

These keywords are handled automatically by the carrier. Once STOP is received, no further messages will be sent to that number until UNSTOP is received.

## Data Privacy

- No message content is stored beyond 30 days
- No third-party data sharing
- No marketing or promotional content
- All messages are personal assistant communications (reminders, scheduling, coordination)
- Opt-in consent records are stored securely and are not shared with third parties
